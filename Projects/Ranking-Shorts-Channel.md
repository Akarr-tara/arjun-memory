*Source: Content creation chat*

## Channel Format
- 5-short series per topic: 4 real/credible rankings + 1 absurd AI pick designed to bait comments
- AI pick should be confidently wrong, not random — triggers a specific fanbase
- Label the AI pick on screen: "AI ranked this #1 💀"
- Place AI pick at #1 or #2 position, not buried

## What Makes It Work
- 4 real picks keep the video credible, making the bait pick land harder
- Comment debate = engagement signal = more views
- Formula works best in: sports, food, gaming, music
