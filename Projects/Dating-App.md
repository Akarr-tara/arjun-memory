# Values-Based Dating App — Project Idea

*Source: Business ideation chat*
*Links: [[AI-Agency]] [[Profile]] [[University-Decision]]*
*Status: Ideation phase — not yet active*

## Core Concept
- Values-based dating app — matches people on AI-extracted insights, not looks
- Users link their existing AI (Claude, ChatGPT) which already knows them deeply
- AI extracts: insecurities, needs, dating intent, shared trauma, personality, attachment style
- App matches on that data — won't reveal exactly what drove the match
- Key differentiator: uses existing unfiltered AI chat history, not fresh onboarding interviews

## Market Research
- Competitors exist but none have nailed execution:
  - Known (SF) — voice AI interviews, no swiping
  - AURA — AI Personality DNA + gradual blur reveal
  - SciMatch — facial personality inference, poor execution
- ~80% of users prefer personality-based matching over looks (eHarmony stat)
- Demand validated — execution gap exists

## Strategic Decisions
- Launch market: Singapore — dense, dating-app-friendly, lower competition
- International expansion needed sooner given Singapore's ceiling
- Patent shelved — matching algorithms hard to patent; trade secret + speed + brand trust better early protection
- First cohort (50–200 users): community outreach over paid marketing

## Key Risks
- Sensitive personal data (trauma, insecurities) = PDPA compliance required before storing/processing
- High trust barrier — linking private AI conversations is a big ask
- Privacy safeguard: process data client-side or via AI API so only derived scores/tags reach servers

## Recommended Build Sequence
1. Define values/needs/trauma taxonomy — hardest intellectual problem, do first
2. Validate matching logic manually on small cohort (~20 people, Google Form)
3. Automate AI-linking mechanism
4. Harden security/privacy architecture
5. Scale marketing only after matches proven to produce good real-world dates
