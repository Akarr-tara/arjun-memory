## Goal
Make money from an AI agency as a side hustle

## Constraints
- Only a few hours a week available
- Side hustle level, not full-time

## Three Paths Compared

### Done-For-You Automation (Best Fit)
- Tools: Make/n8n/Zapier + AI APIs
- Services: chatbots, lead-qual workflows, automated support
- Reusable templates = low overhead
- Best fit for limited hours

### AI Content/Marketing Agency
- More time-hungry per client
- Harder to scale part-time

### AI Consulting/Strategy
- Highest rate potential
- Needs existing credibility/network
- Tough to bootstrap from zero

## Decision
