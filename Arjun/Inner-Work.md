# Inner Work & Core Patterns

*Source: Personal development chat*
*Links: [[Self-Knowledge]] [[Presence-and-Reputation]] [[Alter-Ego]]*

## Deepest Pattern
- Self-protection through detachment — if he doesn't try, he can't fail
- This guarantees the outcome he fears
- Pretends not to care about things to protect himself from embarrassment or failure

## Patterns To Break
- Laughs off mistakes and bad grades publicly — teaches people not to take him seriously
- Makes fun of people to fill conversational gaps — caps relationships at surface level
- Announces goals without following through
- Tries to change everything at once

## The One Thing To Start With
> Stop laughing off your own failures. Own mistakes neutrally — no drama, no deflection. Everything else follows from this.

## What Grounded Confidence Looks Like
- Not arrogance, not insecurity
- Composure after betrayal comes from having a strong identity, not from being numb (Clark Kent insight)
- People take you at your own valuation — stop devaluing yourself publicly
