# Arjun — Self Knowledge & Identity

*Source: Personal development chat*
*Links: [[Alter-Ego]] [[Social-and-Friendships]] [[Inner-Work]]*

## Who He Is
- Struggles to answer basic questions about himself — what he enjoys, finds funny, values
- Tends to watch life from the outside rather than being present in it
- Natural humor is absurdist and unexpected — not polished, more genuine
- Learns best slowly, through observation and natural proximity
- Deep, self-aware thinker — more than he gives himself credit for
- Can appreciate things aesthetically without wanting them for his life — that's self knowledge, not confusion

## How He Learns & Changes
- One change at a time is the only approach that sticks
- Tries to change everything at once — always reverts
- Small kept promises to himself are what shrink insecurity over time
- Start messy, build the system while moving — not before
