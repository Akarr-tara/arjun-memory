# Presence & Reputation

*Source: Personal development chat*
*Links: [[Inner-Work]] [[Social-and-Friendships]] [[Alter-Ego]]*

## Current Reality
- Currently discounted in group settings — people don't pay attention
- Root cause: laughing things off, not following through, performing instead of being real

## The Insight
- Presence is the one thing that separates two equally wealthy men
- The magnetic one isn't needing anything from the room
- People take you at your own valuation — stop devaluing yourself publicly

## What To Build
- Grounded confidence — knows what he wants, doesn't need validation
- Owns mistakes neutrally — no drama, no deflection
- Stops performing to be liked
