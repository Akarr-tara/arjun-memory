# Social Life & Friendships

*Source: Personal development chat*
*Links: [[Self-Knowledge]] [[Girls-and-Relationships]] [[Inner-Work]]*

## Pattern
- Only made 3 real friends in 10 years — both through fresh starts or natural proximity
- Spreading thin chasing breadth led to emptiness — depth over breadth always
- Better one on one than in groups
- Performing to be liked is exhausting and keeps people from knowing the real him

## How Friendships Actually Form For Him
- Repeated proximity
- Letting curiosity build naturally
- Not forcing it
- Basic training / new environments = fresh starts — use them

## With Older People
- Struggles — needs reps
- Curiosity is the key tool
