# Alter Ego & Mindset

## Concept
Arjun is building an alter ego — a version of himself that is:
- Responsible and disciplined
- Respectable in all environments
- Financially intelligent
- Physically and mentally sharp
- Culturally connected (Punjabi heritage)

## Mindset Principles
- Build systems, not just habits
- Think in leverage — what gives the most return per unit of effort
- Delayed gratification — shift free time is investment time
- Everything is a skill that can be learned
- Identity drives behaviour — become the person first, actions follow
