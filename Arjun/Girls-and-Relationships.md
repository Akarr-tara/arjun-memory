## Key Lessons
- Fumbled a girl from Penang — great natural conversations died when he started overthinking
- Move things forward while energy is there — don't wait for the perfect moment
- Stronger online than face to face — needs to get comfortable being unpolished in person
- Wants to love deeply but fears being destroyed by heartbreak

## The Right Framework
- Avoid ranking women — think compatibility not quality
- Experience with girls matters not for technique but for lowering stakes in his head
- Wants to attract the right girl by becoming the right person — correct instinct
- Working hard on something makes you unconsciously more attractive
