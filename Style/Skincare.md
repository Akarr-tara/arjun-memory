# Skincare Routine

*Source: Looksmaxxing chat*
*Links: [[Looksmaxxing]] [[Profile]]*

## Skin Profile
- Mild acne, uneven texture, oiliness, dark circles, dry lips
- Key preference: products must feel like nothing — gel and water-based only

## Morning Routine
1. Gentle gel cleanser
2. Niacinamide serum (The Ordinary)
3. Biore UV Aqua Rich SPF 50 — feels like water, specifically chosen

## Night Routine
1. Oil cleanser first
2. Gel face wash
3. BHA/salicylic acid 2–3x per week (texture and pores)
4. Can skip moisturiser at night given oiliness preference

## Priority Order
SPF → Niacinamide → consistent double cleanse at night

## Lips
- Issue: hyperpigmentation — darker outer edges, lighter centre
- Caused by sun exposure, dryness, dead skin buildup
- Fix: lip scrub 2–3x per week, Vitamin C lip treatment nightly, SPF lip balm every morning
- Timeline: 4–6 weeks for visible results

## Teeth
- Currently using Colgate Optic White — good start
- Next step: Crest 3D Whitestrips (Shopee/Lazada)
- Best option: professional in-chair whitening ($300–800 SG)
- Goal: warm natural white, not artificial blue-white — suits olive skin

## Body Odour
- Odour comes from bacteria converting sweat, not sweat itself
- Active ingredient needed: chloroxylenol (Dettol)
- Best approach: antibacterial body wash on key areas + antiperspirant applied at night
- Recommended: Dove Men+ or Old Spice applied at night for best absorption in SG humidity
