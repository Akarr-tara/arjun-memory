*Source: Looksmaxxing chat*

## Face & Physical Features
- Face shape: Oval/diamond — cheekbones are widest point
- Strongest features: cheekbones (8.5/10), eyes (8/10), long eyelashes
- Areas to improve: jawline (6/10), nose (6.5/10)
- Biggest quick win: groom eyebrows + tighten beard lines

## Hair
- Recommended cut: textured layered medium length with curtain fringe
- Fringe frames face, layers reduce bulk, blended sides clean up flyaways
- Natural wave is an asset — right cut lets it work with you

## Turban
- Current: navy, good colour, good width
- Issues: sits too low/flat, covers too much forehead, covers ears almost entirely
- Ideal: moderate height, slightly rounded top, half ear coverage, pulled back to show more forehead

## Outfit Styling With Turban
- Slim/tailored fits work far better than baggy with a turban
- Turban = statement piece — keep outfit simple around it
- Best colours for olive skin: burgundy, forest green, mustard, navy, burnt orange, charcoal, cream
- Avoid: neon, pastels, cool-toned colours

## Attractiveness Gap Analysis
- Structural advantages: strong cheekbones, defined facial structure, skin smoothness
- Gap is almost entirely fixable — skin routine + curtain fringe haircut closes most of it
