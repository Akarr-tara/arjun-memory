# Sub-Agent System

## Concept
- One main AI Arjun talks to
- Multiple specialist agents running in background
- Agents batch their questions → route to main AI
- Main AI asks Arjun everything at once
- Arjun can request new agents be created anytime

## Planned Agents
1. Day Planner — PRIORITY, reads Google Calendar, builds daily schedule
2. Fitness / Physique — training plans around shift schedule
3. Looksmaxxing — appearance, skincare, grooming
4. Meal Planning — nutrition, meal prep
5. AI Stylist — outfit suggestions from wardrobe photos
6. University — degree and course decisions
7. Personality Development — mindset, communication, confidence
8. Language Learning — Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi
9. Finance & Investing — wealth building, tax strategy, investing
10. Alter Ego & Mindset — persona development, mental frameworks

## AI Stylist Agent Detail
- Photograph all clothes and accessories once
- Store images in memory
- Suggest outfits based on: skin colour, body shape, formality, weather, occasion
